# Privacy Policy

This application is a personal-use tool operated by a single individual
for exporting their own Oura Ring data. It is not offered as a service
to the public.

## Data accessed
Only the Oura API data of the account owner: sleep, readiness, activity,
heart rate, workouts, sessions, tags and SpO2.

## Data storage
All retrieved data is stored locally on the operator's own computer.
Nothing is transmitted to, stored on, or shared with any third party.
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

## Data retention
Data remains on the operator's local machine and may be deleted at any
time by the operator.

## Third parties
No data is shared with any third party.
